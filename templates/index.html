What makes DocSafe different from normal LLMs?

Note: While most LLM services claim not to store or process personally identifiable information (PII), in practice, users are often denied service when attempting to submit highly sensitive data, and many users—unaware of these risks—still share confidential documents seeking help. DocSafe bridges this critical gap by providing an automated, enterprise-grade solution that sanitizes and anonymizes all sensitive information before it ever reaches the LLM, ensuring no PII is leaked, no matter the user’s awareness or intent. With DocSafe, organizations and individuals can safely leverage the power of LLMs for even the most sensitive tasks, with full confidence in privacy, compliance, and security.
